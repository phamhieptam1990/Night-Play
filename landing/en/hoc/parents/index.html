Parents’ questions

Is the parent area meant for kids?

No. It sits behind an adult sum. Keep the device somewhere you can keep an eye on while your child learns.

What if I switch phones?

Progress lives only on the device, so a new phone starts fresh. Parent accounts for syncing are planned for later.

What does accuracy on the first try mean?

The share of questions answered correctly on the first choice, not counting retries after a hint.

Does my child lose the round when time is up?

No. They can finish the 5-question round in progress before the app rests.
