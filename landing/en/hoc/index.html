How kids learn

  1. Open and play

    The first question appears right away, no long menus. Subject and grade are remembered.

  2. Hints, not answers

    On a mistake the mascot asks hint by hint so the child works it out.

  3. Practise the gap

    The app logs thinking errors and serves more of exactly that type.

Features

Learn like playing, understand like a tutor sat beside you

Every topic is its own game. Most of the time is spent solving; decorations are just rewards.

🎮

Topic mini-games

Each topic has its own rules, not a “question + 4 buttons” quiz in disguise.

🧠

Step-by-step AI tutor

The AI never just hands over the answer. It asks hint by hint so the child works it out. Kids pick or type an answer; there is no free chat.

🎯

Adaptive practice

Every wrong answer maps to a specific thinking error, so the app knows where the gap is and practises exactly that.

🐾

Mascot the companion

the mascot cheers right answers, encourages after mistakes and remembers the errors your child tends to make.

🗺️

Adventure map

Each topic is a land. Finish it to unlock the next, with a new story chapter every week.

🏘️

Pets and a village

Stars earned while learning buy decorations and build a custom character. A reason to open the app every day.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What age is it for?

Fun Learning is for primary students, Grades 1 to 5, plus 6th-grade entrance exam prep. Content is organised by grade.

Does the AI tutor just give the answer?

No. While practising, the AI only gives step-by-step hints so the child answers on their own. Children do not free-chat with the AI.

Does it cost money?

You can try it right away. Plans are monthly or yearly; plan details and prices are shown clearly before payment.

Where do the questions come from?

The curriculum follows Vietnam’s 2018 national curriculum (Circular 32/2018/TT-BGDĐT). We write and review the questions ourselves and do not copy textbook content.

Is my child’s data protected?

There are no ads and no free chat. See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
