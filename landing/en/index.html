Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Free to play every game, no account. Night Pass is an optional plan unlocking extra modes, difficulties and special looks in each game.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open nightplay.vn in your browser; you can add it to the home screen.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after one online visit. Server features (Night Pass check, leaderboard, play-for-me) need a connection.

Is it safe for my child?

Pop Play has no accounts, ads or name fields; parents set time limits and lock the screen. Please stay with young children.

What data do you collect?

Anonymous counts while playing; an email only if you buy Night Pass. See the Privacy Policy.

Does Night Pass auto-renew?

No. When it ends you return to Free; nothing is charged automatically.

How many devices?

Up to 3 per plan; to move, restore with your email (OTP).

What are play-for-me and challenges?

Play-for-me: a friend continues your round via a link. Challenge: a link lets a friend play the same round.

How do refunds work?

See the refund section of the Terms or email support@nightplay.vn.

How do I limit my child's play time?

Open Pop Play, hold the parent lock button for 3 seconds and answer the adults-only question. In the Parents panel, pick a limit from 15 to 120 minutes a day and switch games on or off. When time is up, the game stops and your child is reminded to rest.
